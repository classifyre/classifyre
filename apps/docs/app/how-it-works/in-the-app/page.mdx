# A Tour of the App

Classifyre's left-hand navigation is organised around one journey: **connect
systems, scan them, review what was found, and investigate what matters**. This
page walks through every screen in that order — what it shows, what you do
there, and where the deeper documentation lives.

---

## Workspaces

**The screen before every other screen.** Classifyre opens on the workspace
directory: a card for each isolated workspace on this instance, with its name,
description, any links you have pinned to it, and a live count of sources and
failing sources. Cards are grouped under **category** headings, so a directory
of sixty stays readable. Pick one to enter it — everything documented below
happens inside the workspace you opened.

Use *New workspace* to add one, the gear on a card for its settings, categories,
links and URL slug, *Categories* to manage the shelves themselves, and *All
workspaces* at the top of the sidebar to come back and switch. Inside a
workspace, the line under its name in the sidebar names the category it is filed
under and links to everything filed alongside it.

Learn more: **[Workspaces](/how-it-works/workspaces/)** — what is isolated, how
addressing works, how categories and links are organised, and what governance
the enterprise offering adds.

## Discovery

**Your at-a-glance overview.** Discovery (*Overview* in the sidebar) is the
landing dashboard, in three rows:

- **The brief** — total findings over the last 7, 30, or 90 days, what needs
  attention broken down by priority, and what has arrived today, this week and
  this month. Review state (open, resolved, not relevant, set aside) is a single
  muted line: it is bookkeeping, not the headline.
- **Casework and recent scans** — the cases and inquiries in flight, how many
  matches appeared since you last looked, and the last ten runs.
- **Explore connections** — a search box, your saved views, and the assets worth
  starting from (most critical, most lineage, biggest duplicate patterns, links
  people drew), each one a way into [Explore](/how-it-works/explore/#explore-on-the-overview).

Use it to answer *"what is going on?"* in ten seconds, then click any number to
jump straight to the filtered detail view behind it.

> Priority counts, review counts, cases and top assets are all clickable —
> Discovery is a launchpad, not just a report.

## Explore

**What an asset, a source or a glossary term is connected to, and why.** Every
source and every asset has a **Connections** tab, every glossary term opens on
*Where it is connected*, and duplicate review has an **Explore** section that
draws its pairs, filtered by pattern, kind, score, review state and source: a canvas of what it is connected to, by
lineage, links, duplicates, shared values and meaning, each line saying why.
A source's opens on its most connected assets. Keep every connection, only
those inside the source, or only those leaving it for other sources; double-click
an asset to dive in, and follow the trail back out. Trace lineage upstream,
downstream or both, as far as it goes; badges show what is already in a case or
answers a watch. Send what you found to a case, and draw the links the data does
not know about (they survive every re-scan).

The Explore card on the Overview opens the same canvas on a page of its own,
starting from a search, your saved views, or a table of the assets worth
starting from; a view there can be saved with a name and the layout you gave it.

Learn more: **[Explore: Following Connections](/how-it-works/explore/)**.

## Sources

**The systems you've connected.** Each source is a connection to something you
already run — SharePoint, Confluence, Jira, a database, a file share, a local
folder. Here you add new sources, test their connection, choose which detectors
run on them, and set scan schedules. A source's page has its findings, its
assets, and its **Connections**: what its assets are connected to, inside the
source and in others ([Explore](/how-it-works/explore/#on-a-sources-page)).

Learn more: **[Sources](/sources/)** — including the full
[catalog of source types](/sources/) and
[configuration fields](/sources/configuration/).

## Scans

**Every run, past and present.** A scan is one pass over a source. This screen
shows run history and live progress, counts by status (running, queued,
completed, failed), and trends over time. You can also start a scan manually
with the scan wizard.

Learn more: **[Scans](/flow/)** — the journey of a scan, its phases, and what
repeat scans do.

## Assets

**Everything your scans have found.** Every document, page, file, or record a
scan registers becomes an asset with metadata — owner, location, when it last
changed. The Assets screen is the inventory: filter by new, updated, or
unchanged, and see which assets carry the most findings. Each asset's page has
its findings and a **Connections** tab: Explore's canvas starting from that
asset, with its lineage, duplicates, shared values and concepts.

Learn more: **[Assets & Metadata](/sources/assets-and-metadata/)**.

## Findings

**The signals worth acting on.** Whenever a detector spots something — a leaked
secret, personal data, a tagged term, a value out of range — it raises a
finding with a priority and a confidence. This screen is where you triage:
filter by priority, source,
or status, and resolve, ignore, or mark false positive in bulk. Your decisions
stick across future scans.

Learn more: **[Findings & Results](/detectors/findings/)** and
**[Detectors](/detectors/)**.

## Duplicate review

**The same thing, seen everywhere.** Assets that share concrete values — an
email address, an ID, a name — or that read as near-duplicate text are matched
into pairs and clusters.

The screen is a **queue, not a graph**: matches are grouped by *why* they
matched, patterns are ranked by how much work one decision settles, and you work
through pairs with the keyboard until it's empty. What you confirm can go
straight into a case or an inquiry.

Learn more: **[Duplicate Review](/duplicates/)** and the plain-English
[Connections & Duplicates](/how-it-works/duplicates-and-similarity/).

## Investigations

**Where findings become answers.** Two tools live here: **inquiries** — saved
questions that keep watching for matching findings — and **cases** — structured
case files where you collect evidence, weigh hypotheses, build a timeline, and
write a conclusion.

Learn more: **[Investigations](/investigations/)**,
**[Inquiry](/investigations/inquiry/)**, and
**[Cases](/investigations/cases/)**.

## Glossary

**Your shared vocabulary.** The glossary holds the people, organisations,
locations, and terms that matter to your organisation, with their aliases. It
keeps humans and AI agents talking about the same entities — and when the
autopilot proposes a new term or alias, you review and verify it here.

The page opens on the **meaning map**: every concept grouped by scheme, sized
by the evidence behind it, with what turns up together. Select a concept to
watch it, bring it into a case or go to its evidence; add, edit and relate
concepts right on the map. The lists (concepts, entities, schemes, unbound
vocabulary, proposals) are the tabs next to it.

Learn more: **[Glossary & Shared Vocabulary](/how-it-works/glossary/)**.

## Harness

**Mission control for the AI autopilot.** The harness shows everything the
autopilot does: a live activity timeline, per-run flight recordings with the
reasoning behind each decision, token usage, the agents and tools available,
and what the system has learned. Its **Configuration** tab also manages AI
providers and the independent Assistant/Harness assignments, along with the
Harness's external MCP tools.

Learn more: **[Autopilot](/investigations/autopilot/)** and its
[flight recorder](/investigations/autopilot/flight-recorder/) and
[steering](/investigations/autopilot/steering/) pages, plus the
[AI Providers guide](/settings/ai-providers/).

## Sandbox

**A safe place to experiment.** Sandbox runs scans in isolation, without
touching your real findings — useful for trying out a new source configuration
or detector before turning it on for real.

## Notifications

**The events worth telling you about.** Failed scans, sudden spikes or drops in
findings, recoveries, and first-scan completions land here (and in the bell at
the top right). Filter by priority or type, mark items important, and jump
straight to the affected scan or finding.

Learn more: **[Notifications](/notifications/)**.

## Settings

**Workspace-wide configuration.** Software version, scan capacity, language and
timezone, the MCP server that lets your own tools query Classifyre, Telegram
and Slack bots, and portable workspace export/import archives.

Learn more: **[Settings](/settings/)**,
**[MCP Server](/settings/mcp-server/)**.
