# Leads

A **lead** is something the case suggests it may be missing: a document that
looks like a piece of your evidence, an important new answer of one of its
watches, a finding similar to one already in the case, or something
[Autopilot](/investigations/autopilot/) wants a person to check.

Nothing joins the case by itself. You **accept** a lead, or drag it onto the
board, and it becomes evidence. Or you **dismiss** it, and it is never suggested
for this case again.

Open the **Leads** panel from the rail on the right of the board. Its badge
counts the leads waiting for you.

## Where leads come from

Every lead carries its reason. The panel names it on each card and lets you
filter by it.

| Reason | What it is | Comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Look-alike** | A document that is an identical copy of a piece of evidence, or shares most of its key values: the same account numbers, e-mail addresses, names | [Duplicate detection](/duplicates/) |
| **Watch answer** | An important answer of a watch linked to the case (importance 85 or more) that is not in the case yet | The case's [watches](/investigations/inquiry/) |
| **Similar** | A finding whose content is close to a finding in the case | [Embeddings](/settings/embeddings/) |
| **Same value** | A similar finding that carries *the very same value* as one in the case, in another document. The same IBAN turning up elsewhere is a thread to pull, not just a resemblance, so it is named apart | Embeddings |
| **Autopilot** | Something the case agent found and wants a person to check, with its reasoning | [Autopilot](/investigations/autopilot/) |
| **Bookmarked** | A finding someone put on the list by hand, through MCP or the API | People |

Similar and same-value leads need embeddings. While
[embeddings are off](/settings/cleanup/), the panel says so, and look-alikes and
watch answers still come in.

## They refresh by themselves

You don't have to ask for leads. The case looks for new ones:

- whenever its **evidence changes**: something added on the board, a watch's
  auto-add, a lead accepted, a finding attached from anywhere;
- whenever a **watch** is linked or gets new answers;
- whenever a **finding filter** is added or changed, so leads it rules out go
  away;
- when you **open the board**, at most every ten minutes.

A burst of changes counts as one refresh, which runs in the background a few
seconds after the last one. The panel picks up new leads within a minute.
**Refresh** at the top of the panel looks right away.

Each refresh proposes at most **ten leads of each kind**, so one kind never
crowds out the others, and a case holds at most **60 leads waiting for
review**. When it is full, review some to make room. A closed case gets no new
leads.

The case also keeps its list honest:

- a lead whose finding or document joined the case another way (added from the
  board, brought in by a watch) moves to **Reviewed** as **Already in the
  case**;
- a lead whose finding was resolved, deleted or filtered out, or whose document
  is gone from its source, disappears. If the finding comes back, it can be
  suggested again.

## Reading a lead

| Part | Example | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| **Reason** | **LOOK-ALIKE** | Why the case suggests it (see above). Confirmed duplicates and same-value leads have a darker outline |
| **Score** | *Match 80%* · *Importance 91* · *87% similar* · *Identical* | How strong the reason is: a look-alike's match weight, a finding's importance, or its similarity |
| **Severity** | **HIGH** | The finding's severity (not shown for documents) |
| **What** | `IBAN DE44 5001…` or a document's name | The finding and its value, or the document |
| **Where** | *in payments-2024.xlsx · Accounting* | The document it is in and its source |
| **Why** | *↳ Answer to the watch "Payments to offshore accounts" · new* | What in the case it relates to |

At the foot of the card:

- **Accept** adds it to the case.
- **Dismiss** takes it off the list for good.
- **⌖** flies the board to the evidence the lead relates to, so you can judge it
  in context.
- **Open the watch** (watch answers) opens the **Watches** panel.

## Accepting a lead

There are two ways, and both are the same decision:

- **Accept** on the card. The new evidence lands **next to what it relates to**
  (right of the document it copies, or the finding it resembles), and the board
  flies to it.
- **Drag the card onto the board.** It is accepted right where you drop it.

Either way the lead leaves the list at once, the badge goes down, and the
[timeline](/investigations/cases/timeline/) records who accepted it.

What joins the case depends on the kind of lead:

| Lead | What is added |
|---|---|
| A finding (watch answer, similar, same value, Autopilot, bookmarked) | The finding, attached to the case. Its document comes with it if it was not on the board yet; if it was, the finding joins it there |
| A look-alike document | The document, as evidence. Its findings wait around it as **+n** ghosts, to [attach](/investigations/cases/evidence/#attaching-and-detaching-findings) one by one |

An accepted lead is treated like any other finding that arrives: a case-wide
[escalation rule](/investigations/cases/escalation/) that matches marks it
straight away.

## Dismissing a lead

**Dismiss** asks for a reason. The reason is optional, and it teaches
Autopilot what does not belong in this case. A dismissed lead is never
suggested for this case again, by the case or by Autopilot.

Under the list, **Dismiss all *n* shown** dismisses everything the current
filters show. For example, after checking the two *Similar* leads that matter,
dismiss the rest in one go.

The **Reviewed** section at the bottom lists what was accepted and dismissed,
by whom and when, and the leads that joined the case another way.

## Finding your way in a long list

<LeadFilters />

- **Reason chips** show how many leads of each kind wait. Click one to show only
  those, and click it again for all.
- **Search** filters by value, document, source, and what the lead relates to.
- **Sort** by **Most relevant** (confirmed and identical copies first, then by
  score) or **Newest**.

## Leads and Duplicate review

Look-alike leads come from the same engine as [Duplicate review](/duplicates/),
and the two agree:

- a pair you marked **Not a duplicate** or **Split** is never suggested;
- a pair you **confirmed** comes first and says *Confirmed a duplicate … in
  Duplicate review*. When you accept it, its decision shows the case under
  **Used in**, so it no longer counts as *went nowhere yet* in
  [Decisions](/duplicates/decisions/);
- turning duplicate detection off in [Features](/settings/cleanup/) stops
  look-alike leads.

The other direction still works too: **Add to case** in Decisions brings
confirmed pairs into a case in bulk.

## Leads, neighbours and connections

A copy of your evidence can show up twice: as a lead, and as a
[suggested neighbour](/investigations/cases/connections/#suggested-neighbours)
on the canvas. The two do different jobs:

| | **Suggested neighbours** | **Leads** |
|---|---|---|
| **Where** | On the canvas, around your evidence | In the Leads panel |
| **What** | Assets connected to the evidence: lineage, links, duplicates, similar | Findings and documents worth adding, each with a reason and a score |
| **Decision** | None. Add one with **+**, or hide it until you reload | Accept or dismiss. Dismissals are remembered for the case |
| **Best for** | Seeing the neighbourhood while you work | Working through what the case may be missing |

Adding a neighbour that is also a lead settles the lead: it moves to
**Reviewed** as **Already in the case**.

## Leads and watches

A watch with **auto-add on** brings its new answers straight into the case, so
they never become leads. With **auto-add off**, new answers wait in the
**Watches** panel, and the important ones also appear as leads, so they do not
drown among the rest.

[Finding filters](/investigations/cases/cleanup/#finding-filters) apply to leads
as they do to watches: a kind of finding the case filters out is not suggested,
and waiting leads it matches go away when the filter is added. A lead you
bookmarked yourself stays; picking something by hand is your call.

---

## Leads and Autopilot

Autopilot proposes a lead instead of attaching a finding it has not verified,
and writes its reasoning into the lead. It checks the list first and never
proposes something that was dismissed. It does not need to ask for refreshes:
the case does that by itself.

Over [MCP](/settings/mcp-server/), the same list is available to any client:
`list_case_leads`, `review_case_lead`, `propose_case_lead` and
`generate_case_leads`.

<Callout type="info">
**In the timeline.** Automatic refreshes appear as **one** *New leads* entry
per stretch of refreshes, by *the case's own lead refresh*. It says how many
leads of each kind arrived and names a few. A refresh you ask for is an entry
under your name. Every accept and dismiss is its own entry, with the reason
when one was given.
</Callout>
