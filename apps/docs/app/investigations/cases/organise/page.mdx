# Notes, frames & comments

Evidence and hypotheses are the substance of a case. Notes, frames, comments and
highlights are how you **organise** it: what to do next, which things belong
together, what a colleague should look at, and the story in the order it
happened.

## Sticky notes

A note is free text on the board: a question, a fact you confirmed by phone, a
to-do, a warning.

- **Place one**: press `N` and click on the board, right-click the canvas →
  **Add note here**, or press `⌘K` → **New note**.
- **Write**: double-click the note. **Markdown works**: `**bold**`, lists,
  headings and links. Press `Esc` or click outside to save.
- **Colour**: right-click → **Colour**: yellow, blue, green, pink or grey. A
  colour code shared by the team (say, green for confirmed facts and pink for
  risks) makes a busy board easy to read.
- **Resize**: select the note and drag its edges.
- **Link it** like anything else: pull a line from one of its ports to the
  evidence it is about.
- **Duplicate**: `⌘D`, or right-click → **Duplicate**.

<Callout type="info">
Two people editing the same note at the same moment never overwrite each other.
The second save is refused, the board says who changed the note in the
meantime, and your text is copied to the clipboard so you can merge it.
</Callout>

## Frames

A frame is a named, tinted area that **groups** things: a timeline of one day,
everything about one person, the exfiltration path, the evidence still to
review.

- **Draw one**: press `F` and drag a rectangle on the board. Or `⌘K` → **New
  frame**.
- **Name it**: double-click the title bar.
- **Put things in**: drag them into the frame, or right-click them → **Move to
  frame**. Whatever is in a frame **moves with it**. Drag the frame by its title
  bar.
- **Take things out**: drag them out, or **Move to frame → No frame**.
- **Tint**: right-click → **Tint**: grey, yellow, blue, green, pink or violet.
- **Collapse**: the arrow in the title bar folds the frame down to its title and
  shows how many items it holds. Expand it again the same way.
- **Resize**: select it and drag its edges.

Deleting a frame keeps what was inside it: the items stay on the board where
they were.

## Comments

Comments are conversations **pinned** to a spot: an asset, a single finding, or
a point on the board. They are how a team talks about the case without leaving
it.

- **Start one**: press `C` and click where the comment belongs. Or right-click
  something → **Comment**.
- **Reply**: click the pin to open the conversation and type a reply.
- **Resolve**: when the question is answered, **Resolve** greys the pin. It is
  never deleted, so the conversation stays on record. **Reopen** brings it back.
- Resolved comments are hidden unless you switch on **View → Comments → Show
  resolved comments**.

A pin moves with whatever it is pinned to. Every comment conversation is also a
*discussion* thread: the **Timeline** panel's *Threads* tab lists them all.

## Highlights

Right-click anything → **Highlight** and pick a marker colour: yellow, green,
blue, pink or red. **None** takes it off. A highlighted asset or card gets a
coloured ring; a highlighted finding gets a coloured halo.

Highlights are for your eyes and your team's: mark what is confirmed, what is
disputed, what belongs to one person. Then **View → Highlight by → Only
highlighted** shows just those and fades the rest.

**Highlight by** can also light up everything from one **source**, one
**detector** or one **hypothesis**, without you marking anything by hand.

## Keeping the board tidy

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Tidy up** (**More** menu, or right-click the canvas) | Lays everything out automatically, left to right along the lines. One step, so **Undo** restores your layout |
| **Collapse findings** (`E`) | Folds a busy asset's findings into a ring of severity colours |
| **Collapse all** (**View**) | Folds every asset at once; **Expand all** unfolds them |
| **Bring to front / Send to back** | Decides what sits on top where things overlap |
| **Remove card from board** | Takes a hypothesis card off the board without deleting the hypothesis |
| **Snapshots** | Keep the board as it is now before a big reorganisation |

<Callout type="info">
Selecting something fades everything that is not connected to it, so even a
large board reads well one piece at a time. For a clean overview, zoom out: the
board switches to shapes and names, then to compact chips. See
[Navigating & shortcuts](/investigations/cases/navigate/#zoom-levels).
</Callout>
