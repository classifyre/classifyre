# Hypothesis rules

A watch that adds its answers to a case by itself fills the board with
evidence. A **hypothesis rule** says what the case thinks of it: *answers of
this watch go to that hypothesis, as supporting, contradicting or neutral*.
From then on, every answer that comes into the case through the watch is:

- **linked to the hypothesis** with the stance you chose, so the hypothesis'
  balance of evidence moves by itself;
- **landed on the board next to the hypothesis**, instead of wherever the
  board happens to have room. If the hypothesis sits **inside a frame**, the
  evidence lands inside that frame (and the frame grows to hold it);
- **written on the timeline**: which watch, which hypotheses, which stance and
  how many findings. A watch that scans every few minutes reads as one growing
  entry.

Rules belong to the **case**, like [finding filters](/investigations/cases/cleanup/#finding-filters).
The watch itself is untouched: the same watch can feed other cases that handle
its answers differently.

---

## Adding a rule

Open the **Watches** panel on the board and pick a watch. Under **Rules** you
find **Link to hypothesis**. Press **+**:

1. **Hypothesis**: one of the case's hypotheses (shown with its colour and its
   H-number on the board).
2. **Stance**: **Supports** (the answer speaks for it), **Contradicts** (it
   speaks against, a reject) or **Neutral** (related, but it does not decide).
3. **Which answers**: **All**, only a **finding type**, or only values that
   match a **pattern** (a regular expression, tested against the matched
   value).
4. **Why** (optional): the reason, shown when you hover the rule.
5. **Also link what the case already holds from this watch**: on by default.
   Links you made by hand are never changed.

One watch can have several rules. An answer can go to several hypotheses with
different stances: the same finding can support one theory and contradict
another.

| You want | Rule |
|---|---|
| Everything the watch finds counts for a theory | **All** → hypothesis, **Supports** |
| Only some answers count against a theory | **A finding type** or **A value pattern** → hypothesis, **Contradicts** |
| Keep related answers on the hypothesis without a verdict | **All** → hypothesis, **Neutral** |

If two rules name the same hypothesis for the same answer, the narrower one
(the one with a finding type or pattern) wins, and between equals the older
one. A rule can't be added twice with another stance: change the first one.

---

## Where evidence lands

New evidence is placed the way the board places things you add yourself, only
anchored to the hypothesis:

| The hypothesis is… | The evidence lands… |
|---|---|
| inside a **frame** | inside the same frame, in the first free spot. A frame that is too small grows. A folded frame stays folded |
| on the open canvas | right of the hypothesis card, on its band, clear of everything else |
| not on the board yet | wherever the board places it when it is opened, as before |

An asset that is already on the board keeps its place; only its new finding is
linked. Nothing you have placed is ever moved.

<Callout type="info">
  An answer linked to hypotheses in two different frames lands in the first
  one. You can drag it anywhere afterwards.
</Callout>

---

## Changing and removing rules

- **Edit** a rule by pressing its hypothesis name: a new stance or a new
  hypothesis **carries the links the rule already made**. A new filter on the
  answers applies from then on.
- **Remove** a rule with **×**. The links it made **stay** by default (they
  become ordinary links of the hypothesis). Tick **Also take away the links
  this rule made** to remove them too. The findings and assets stay in the case
  and on the board either way.
- **Unlink the watch** from the case, and its rules go with it.

---

## Deleting a hypothesis

A hypothesis' rules go with it. Deleting it asks what to do with its evidence:

| Choice | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Keep the evidence in the case** (default) | The findings and assets stay in the case and on the board, without a stance |
| **Also take out its evidence** | The findings and assets linked to it leave the case, except what another hypothesis is also linked to and what carries a note. An asset leaves only when none of its findings stay |

The dialog counts what would leave and what stays before you confirm, and the
timeline records what was taken out.

`update_case_hypothesis_rule` and `remove_case_hypothesis_rule` do the same
