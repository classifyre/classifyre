# Navigating & shortcuts

## The mouse

| Do this | And the board… |
|---|---|
| **Scroll** (or **pinch** on a trackpad) | Zooms in and out around the pointer |
| **Drag the empty canvas** | Draws a selection box: everything it touches is selected |
| **Hold `Space` and drag**, or drag with the **middle button** | Pans around the board |
| **Click** | Selects: a glowing ring, and everything unconnected fades |
| **`⌘`-click** (`Ctrl`-click on Windows) | Adds to the selection, or takes away from it |
| **Double-click** | Opens it in the side panel: details for an asset or finding, the thread for a hypothesis |
| **Right-click** | Everything you can do with it, or on the empty canvas at that spot |
| **Drag** a node | Moves it; a finding keeps the spot you give it |
| **Drag from a port** | Draws a link; see [Lines & links](/investigations/cases/links/#drawing-a-link) |

With the **Pan** tool (`H`), every drag pans and nothing can be picked up by
accident. That's handy on a crowded board.

## Zoom levels

The board changes what it draws as you zoom, so it stays readable at every
distance:

| Level | Zoom | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| **Full detail** | 60% and more | Everything: finding labels, the words along lines, whole hypothesis cards with their tally |
| **Shapes and names** | 30% to 60% | Assets and findings with bigger names; finding labels and the words along lines are hidden (point at a line to read it); cards show their statement |
| **Overview** | under 30% | Compact chips with large names; every asset folds its findings into a ring of severity colours; badges are hidden |

### Fitting and jumping

| Control | Key | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Fit board** | `⇧1` | Zooms to show the whole board |
| **Zoom to selection** | `⇧2` | Zooms to what you selected |
| **Mini-map** | | A small overview of the whole board; drag its frame to jump |
| **Show on board** | | In panels and search results, flies to the item and makes it pulse briefly |

## Search and actions (`⌘K`)

The search box in the top bar (or `⌘K`, `Ctrl K` on Windows) does three jobs at
once: find things **on the board**, find things **anywhere in your data** and
add them, and run **actions**.

| Scope | Finds |
|---|---|
| **All** | The best few of each kind, from the board and from your data, plus matching actions |
| **Board** | Evidence, findings, hypotheses, notes, frames and comments on this board; choose one to fly to it |
| **Corpus** | Assets and findings anywhere in your data; choose one to add it to the board. Things already on the board are tagged **on board** |
| **Actions** | New note, new hypothesis, new frame; open the timeline, leads, case file, evidence table or hypotheses; tidy up; add evidence with filters |

Press `Tab` to switch scope, `↑` `↓` to move, `Enter` to open or add, and
`Esc` to close. If what you need calls for filters, **Search "…" with
filters…** opens the **Add evidence** panel with your words already typed.

## Selecting, spotlighting and focusing

Four ways to cut a busy board down to what matters. Only one is active at a
time:

| | How | What stays lit |
|---|---|---|
| **Selection** | Click something, or drag a box | It and everything directly connected to it (frames stay, as context) |
| **Spotlight** | Click a counter in the top bar: evidence, findings, hypotheses | Everything of that kind |
| **Hypothesis focus** | Click a hypothesis card | The hypothesis, its stance lines and the evidence they point to |
| **Highlight by** | **View → Highlight by** | One source, one detector, one hypothesis, or only highlighted items |

`Esc` unwinds one step at a time: it cancels a tool, a spotlight, an edit, a
focus or a selection first. When nothing is left on the canvas, it closes the
side panel. Press `.` to lock a hypothesis focus so that clicks elsewhere leave
it alone.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| `V` | Select tool |
| `H`, or hold `Space` | Pan |
| `N` | Note tool |
| `F` | Frame tool |
| `T` | Hypothesis tool |
| `C` | Comment tool |
| `L` | Link tool |
| `E` | Fold or unfold the findings of the selected assets |
| `⌘K` | Search, add and act |
| `⌘Z` | Undo |
| `⇧⌘Z` or `⌘Y` | Redo |
| `⌫` or `Delete` | Delete the selected notes, frames, comments, hypothesis cards, links and stances (evidence leaves the case only through **Remove from case…**) |
| `⌘A` | Select everything |
| `⌘D` | Duplicate the selected notes or frames |
| `⇧1` | Fit the board |
| `⇧2` | Zoom to the selection |
| `.` | Lock or unlock the hypothesis focus |
| `Esc` | Clear the tool, selection or focus, then close the side panel |
| `?` | Shortcuts and visual key |

On Windows and Linux, read `Ctrl` for `⌘`.

<Callout type="info">
Press `?` on the board at any time for the **shortcuts and visual key**: every
shortcut, and a key to every line, ring and badge. It is also in the **More**
menu.
</Callout>

## Undo and redo

Every change you make on the board is one step you can take back: moving
things, adding and removing evidence, links, stances, notes, tidy-up, and more.
The **Undo** button's tooltip names the step (*Undo: Move 3 items*). Undo only
takes back your own changes, never a teammate's.
