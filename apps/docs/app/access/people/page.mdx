# People and groups

Everything a tenant admin does happens on Classifyre's **Tenant access** page
(account menu → **Tenant access**, or `/tenant`). It has four tabs:

| Tab          | What you do there                                                                     |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **People**   | Add people by email, put them in groups, give a new temporary password, remove them.  |
| **Groups**   | Create groups, also inside other groups; rename and delete them.                      |
| **Access**   | Give a group a role on the whole tenant, on every workspace a group owns, or on one.  |
| **Activity** | Every change to people, groups, access and workspaces, with who made it and when.     |

The page is only open to **tenant admins**: the people in the tenant's
`admins` group, or anyone else given the _Tenant admin_ role.

<Callout type="info">
  If your company directory owns who is in which group, an operator may have
  turned people management off. People and Groups are then shown read-only
  and are changed in the directory. Access works the same either way.
</Callout>

## A typical setup

<Steps>

### Create the groups

On **Groups**, choose **New group** for each department or team, for example
`finance`. A team inside a department is a group inside a group: open the
department's menu and choose **New group inside**, for example
`finance/managers`.

Being in a group inside another counts as being in both. Someone in
`finance/managers` gets whatever `finance` gets, plus whatever
`finance/managers` gets.

### Add the people

On **People**, choose **Add person** and enter their email, first and last
name.

- For a **new account**, Classifyre shows a **temporary password** once. Give
  it to the person in a safe way; they choose their own password the first
  time they sign in. Classifyre does not keep it: if it is lost, use **New
  temporary password** from the person's menu.
- An **existing account** that is not in any tenant yet, for example one an
  operator made, is added as it is, without a new password.

Then open the person's menu, choose **Change groups** and tick their groups.

### Give the groups access

On **Access**, give each group a role. Common choices:

| Who                               | Role                  | Scope                                    |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Everyone in `finance`             | **Viewer**            | Workspaces owned by `/finance`           |
| `finance/managers`                | **Workspace manager** | Workspaces owned by `/finance`           |
| An auditor group                  | **Auditor**           | The whole tenant                         |

A **workspace manager** can create workspaces for their group and manage
every workspace that group owns, including who has access to it. They cannot
add people to the tenant or change groups; that stays with tenant admins.

</Steps>

Changes take effect at once for the person making them. On a large
installation other servers follow within a minute.

## Removing someone

On **People**, open the person's menu and choose **Remove from tenant**. They
lose every group and every access in the tenant at once, and anything they
are signed in with stops working within a minute.

- An account Classifyre created for them is **deleted** with it.
- An account that came from your company directory or from an operator is only
  removed from the tenant; the account itself stays where it came from.

What they did stays in every workspace's history under their name.

---

## Rules that keep a tenant safe

- **Nobody can remove themselves** from the tenant or from `admins`, and the
  last tenant admin cannot be removed, so a tenant can never lock itself out.
- **The `admins` group cannot be renamed or deleted.** It is how every tenant
  finds its first admins.
- **A group that owns workspaces cannot be deleted**, also when a group inside
  it owns them: those workspaces would belong to nobody. Delete the workspaces
  first. Deleting a group also deletes the access given to it and to its
  workspaces.
- **Someone who already belongs to another tenant cannot be added.** The page
  says only that the address cannot be used, so one tenant never learns who is
  in another. If a person really needs to move, ask an operator.
- **Temporary passwords** are only given to accounts Classifyre created, and
  must be changed at the first sign-in.

---

## What happens where

People, groups and access are all managed here. Behind the scenes, people and
groups are stored in the identity server that runs sign-in, and access
(roles and grants) is stored in Classifyre. You never need the identity
server's own console for day-to-day work; operators use it to set up sign-in,
create tenants and connect company directories (see
[Running Sign-in](/access/operations/)).
