# Roles and rights

A **capability** is one thing Classifyre allows, such as `source.manage`. A
**role** is a fixed list of capabilities. A **grant** gives a **group** a role
on a place: one workspace, the workspaces a group owns, or the whole tenant.
Every API route and every MCP tool checks exactly one capability, so what a
role allows is exactly the list below.

<Callout type="info">
  The tables on this page are generated from the API's own definitions
  (`apps/api/src/identity/roles.ts` and `capabilities.ts`) every time the
  API's client is generated, so they always match what Classifyre enforces.
</Callout>

## The roles

<RoleTable />

In plain words:

| Role                  | For                                                                                                               |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Viewer**            | Reading everything in a workspace, and personal MCP tokens, which can only read too.                              |
| **Investigator**      | A Viewer who also triages findings, tags assets, works on cases and inquiries, reviews leads and asks the assistant. |
| **Curator**           | A Viewer who also writes and approves glossary terms, manages entities and decides duplicates.                    |
| **Source manager**    | Connecting sources, running scans and managing detectors. Sees findings and assets, **not** cases or inquiries.    |
| **Workspace admin**   | Everything in a workspace, settings and access included, except writing code.                                     |
| **Code author**       | An add-on, next to another role: custom source notebooks and code detectors run code on your infrastructure.      |
| **Data reader**       | An add-on, next to Viewer: reading the rows of tables with [SQL](/data-query/). The one role that can also be given on a single source or a single table. |
| **Workspace manager** | Creating workspaces for a group they belong to, and being Workspace admin on every workspace that group owns.     |
| **Auditor**           | The tenant's audit trail and who has access, without seeing findings or content.                                  |
| **Tenant admin**      | Everything in the tenant: every workspace, every grant, the tenant's activity.                                    |

Roles sit side by side; none includes another by name. Someone who needs to
investigate and curate gets both, through two groups or two grants to one
group.

### Roles nobody grants

- **Platform operator** is not a grant. Operators are the people with the
  realm role `classifyre-operator` in the identity server (see
  [The Platform page](/access/operations/#the-platform-page)). They manage
  tenants and, through the identity console, people and groups. They never
  see inside a workspace unless a tenant also gives them a role.
- **Visitor** is what everyone gets on a [public workspace](/access/#public-workspaces)
  they have no grant on: people who have not signed in (first tenant only)
  and members of the tenant. It reads, but not who has access, not the
  tenant's activity, no notifications and no MCP tokens. It is never offered.

## Every capability, by role

● means the role holds the capability. Workspace manager is given on a
group's workspaces or the whole tenant; Auditor and Tenant admin on the whole
tenant only; the others on any place.

## Where a grant applies

| Given on                          | Reaches                                                                    | Where to give it                    |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| One workspace                     | That workspace                                                             | The workspace's **Settings → Access**, or **Tenant access** |
| The workspaces of a group         | Every workspace owned by that group or by a group under it, now and later  | **Tenant access**                   |
| The whole tenant                  | Every workspace of the tenant, and the tenant's own pages                  | **Tenant access**                   |

A grant can end on a date (**Until**). After that it counts for nobody and
stays listed until someone removes it.

## Rules that always hold

- **Access only adds up.** No grant takes anything away.
- **Nobody gives more than they have.** A workspace admin cannot make anyone a
  Code author, because writing code is not theirs to give.
- **Creating a workspace needs membership.** A Workspace manager creates
  workspaces only for a group they are in (or under). Only tenant admins may
  create one for any group of the tenant.
- **MCP tokens act as their creator**, and never beyond what the creator may
  do at the moment the token is used.
