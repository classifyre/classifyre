Trino is the query engine in front of many lakehouses, and one connection to it
reaches every catalog it has configured. Starburst Galaxy and Starburst
Enterprise connect the same way.

## What you need to connect

Host and port of the coordinator, and a user. Sign in with a password, or with
a JWT access token; leave both empty for a cluster with no authentication. Use
HTTPS for anything outside a lab.

## What Classifyre reads

Tables and views in every catalog and schema the user can see, or only the
catalogs and schemas you list. Trino's own `system` catalog and
`information_schema` are skipped. Each table reports its format: `iceberg`,
`delta`, `hudi`, `native` for the engine's own storage, or `external`.

Views and the tables they read are recorded as lineage, parsed from the view

## Worth knowing

- **Each page of rows is one query.** On a busy cluster, a smaller page size or
  a narrower scope keeps the scan light.
- **The format comes from the catalog's connector.** A catalog that federates a
  database, such as PostgreSQL, reports `native`.
