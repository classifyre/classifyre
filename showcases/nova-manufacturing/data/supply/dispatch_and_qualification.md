### Engineering rule ENG-431-D

The sanitary pump P300 accepts housing revision D from suppliers whose SANITARY approval is valid on the material need date. Revision C has a different seal interface; purchasing cannot waive this requirement. All dates quoted in offers are firm destination arrivals.
