### Sampling

Housing batches B994 (Elbe Precision Castings, S001) and B990 (Morava Industrial Parts, S002) were deliberately sampled on both machines, M001 and M002, using the same product and the same inspection method. Each inspected unit appears once. Sample rates are not estimates of a supplier's overall defect rate. No metallography or supplier root-cause report is available yet.

### Scrap rate question

Production switched from mostly P100 to mostly P200 between the two equal-size observation windows. Inspection criteria did not change. Operations asks for an explanation before stopping the line.
