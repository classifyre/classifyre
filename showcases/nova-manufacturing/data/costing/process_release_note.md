# Engineering note ENG-202 — revised cutting process R2

Nova Munich · Monday 5 October 2026, 11:00 UTC
To: Purchasing, Process engineering

Three controlled pilot runs used process R2 with the same material specification and measurement method. Chemistry checks passed.

Engineering approves the pooled pilot yield (total usable mass divided by total input mass) as the planning yield for the next R2 order only, subject to production monitoring. The original quote from Adria Value Metals (S003) of €4.37 per kg plus €0.22 per kg freight remains valid through 9 October. R2 adds no labor, energy, tooling, capacity or setup cost in this comparison.

The 86% yield stays on record as the observation for the previous process and has not been edited. No supplier order has been placed.
