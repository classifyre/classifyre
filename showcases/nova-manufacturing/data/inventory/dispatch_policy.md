# Inventory control policy and open requests

Effective 1 October 2026 · Inventory control, Nova Munich

### Rule

Released, unreserved stock above the safety buffer may move between plants. In our stock extracts, quarantine, reservations and safety quantities are separate buckets and never overlap. The truck departures listed by transport planning are the only slots before the need dates. A destination's requirement is what it still needs, net of other planned receipts.

### Seal cartridges (C100), Munich

Purchasing has an emergency quote of €46 per unit, but no delivery before 23 October. A transfer avoids a planned purchase commitment; its face value is not a realised saving. Lane cost is linear, with no fixed trip charge.

### Resin (CRESIN), Munich

The resin must still be usable on the 6 October consumption date and must travel on a cold-chain route. Reserved resin belongs to a signed customer order and cannot be reassigned.

### Bearing kits (CBRG), Hamburg

Transfer proposals TR51 and TR52 are drafts, not reservations. Both read the Hamburg stock at version 7. An approval has to re-check the aggregate available-to-promise quantity in one transaction, reserve stock once and leave an idempotent decision record. Nobody is permitted to reduce the 40-kit safety buffer.
