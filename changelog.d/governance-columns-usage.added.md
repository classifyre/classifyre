A table's Overview now lists its columns. For each one, a steward can add a business name, a description, a glossary term and a critical-data-element flag. Each column also gets a classification (personal, sensitive, secret and so on), worked out from the findings in that column; a steward confirms it or sets their own. The Overview also shows who read or exported the table in the last 30 days. A new Governance page shows how much of each source is owned and certified, lists the most-read tables that have no owner yet, and lists recent governance changes. Two new MCP tools read an asset's governance and set its owner.
