Overview is now the Dashboard, the page you land on in a workspace. It is about the data you answer for rather than findings and scans: how many assets are governed and certified, how their quality holds, who asks for access and reads them, and what waits on you. It shows your own assets or all of them, over 7, 30 or 90 days, for one source or all. Every number opens the matching assets on the Assets page, which has four tabs over the same asset list, each with the columns and filters that matter there: Raw assets, Governed assets (with how often they are read and which are read most), Shared assets (with their usage and status, each dataset labelled in the list) and Explore. Old Overview links open the Dashboard.
