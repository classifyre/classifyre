The Assets page now has three tabs, shown as cards with their counts: Governance, Raw assets and Shared assets. The tab you pick stays in the address, so a refresh or a shared link opens the same view. The Governance tab is a dashboard to work from, for your own assets or for all of them, over 7, 30 or 90 days and one source or all. It shows access requests waiting for you, which you can approve or decline in place. It also shows failing quality, assets whose governance is unfinished, the assets read most or whose use grew most, quality results per day and per dimension, and coverage per source.
