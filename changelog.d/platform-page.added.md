Operators now manage tenants in Classifyre on a new **Platform** page: create a tenant with its first admin, change who its admins are, bring in workspaces made without sign-in, suspend and resume it, and delete it once it has no workspaces. Operators hold the `classifyre-operator` role, which the Helm chart gives its first admin (`iam.bootstrap.admin.operator`); they see tenants, not what is inside them. People of a suspended tenant see a page saying so.
